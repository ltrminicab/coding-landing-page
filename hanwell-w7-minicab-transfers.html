Discover Hanwell W7 – West London's Green and Historic Neighbourhood

Hanwell W7 is a characterful and well-established west London neighbourhood that sits between the busy commercial centres of Ealing to the east and Southall to the west, offering residents a refreshingly green and community-oriented environment within easy reach of the rest of the capital. The area is particularly celebrated for the magnificent Wharncliffe Viaduct, a Grade I listed railway structure designed by the legendary engineer Isambard Kingdom Brunel for the Great Western Railway in the 1830s, which continues to carry trains across the Brent Valley to this day. Hanwell is also home to the beautiful Brent Lodge Park — affectionately known to locals as Bunny Park — as well as Churchfields Recreation Ground and Boston Manor Park, which together provide some of the most extensive green spaces in the London Borough of Ealing. With Hanwell station now served by the Elizabeth line and Great Western Railway services, and strong bus connections along the Uxbridge Road, residents and visitors enjoy fast and convenient access to Central London, Heathrow Airport, and destinations across the wider Thames Valley.
