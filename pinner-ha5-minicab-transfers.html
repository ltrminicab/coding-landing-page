Discover Pinner HA5 – A Historic Village Suburb in the Heart of the Harrow Countryside

Pinner HA5 is a picturesque and well-regarded residential postcode in the London Borough of Harrow, widely considered one of the most characterful and historically intact village suburbs remaining within Greater London. The postcode spans from the Metropolitan line station at Pinner village in the west through the residential streets of Hatch End and Headstone toward the Eastcote boundary to the south, taking in a wide range of property types from Edwardian terraces and 1930s semis near the High Street to larger detached houses on quiet lanes such as Waxwell Lane, Elm Park Road, and West End Lane toward the Northwood Hills boundary. The postcode's character is shaped by its unusually well-preserved medieval church of St John the Baptist, its traditional High Street with timber-framed buildings, and its annual Pinner Fair — one of the oldest charter fairs in England — all of which give HA5 a local identity distinct from most other outer London postcodes.

Pinner station on the Metropolitan line provides Zone 5 Underground services into Baker Street and the City, making the postcode a practical commuter base for professionals working in central London who also benefit from its quiet residential streets, green open spaces, and easy road access westbound to Heathrow via the A40 at Hillingdon. North Harrow station, also on the Metropolitan line, sits on the eastern edge of HA5 providing an alternative Underground connection into the Harrow on the Hill interchange.
