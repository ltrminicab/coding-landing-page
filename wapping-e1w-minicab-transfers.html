Discover Wapping E1W – London's Most Historic Riverside Quarter

Wapping E1W is one of London's most evocative and historically rich riverside neighbourhoods, occupying a sweeping stretch of the north bank of the Thames between Tower Bridge and Limehouse. Once the beating heart of London's maritime trade, Wapping was the principal docking area for merchant ships arriving in the capital for centuries, and its extraordinary past is written across every cobbled lane, converted warehouse, and riverside terrace that survives today. The legendary Execution Dock, where pirates including the infamous Captain Kidd were hanged and displayed as a warning to passing vessels, stood on Wapping's foreshore for over 400 years. Today the area has been transformed into one of east London's most desirable residential addresses, with former Victorian warehouses and dockside storage buildings converted into striking loft apartments and luxury flats that command some of the finest Thames views in the capital. Tobacco Dock, the remarkable Grade I listed former storage facility, now serves as a contemporary events and exhibition venue, while Wapping High Street is lined with characterful riverside pubs, independent restaurants, and galleries. The Prospect of Whitby on Wapping Wall, reputedly London's oldest surviving riverside tavern dating to 1520, remains one of the neighbourhood's most celebrated landmarks, attracting visitors from across the world who come to experience its extraordinary history and panoramic river views.
