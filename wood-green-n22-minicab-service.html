Discover Wood Green N22 – North London's Busy High Road Community

Wood Green is a different kind of North London neighbourhood from its leafier neighbours to the west. There is nothing quiet about the N22 High Road on a Saturday morning and that is very much the point. The area has genuine energy to it, built over decades by one of London's most diverse populations. Greek Cypriot, Turkish, and West African communities have shaped the food culture along the High Road and the stretch of Green Lanes running south toward Turnpike Lane, where the bakeries, meyhanes, and fishmongers have been trading for generations. The Mall Wood Green, the large indoor shopping centre on the High Road, draws shoppers from right across Haringey and beyond. Noel Park, a few streets east of the tube station, is a remarkably intact Victorian planned housing estate built in the 1880s and now a conservation area. Alexandra Palace sits a short uphill walk to the south-west, visible from much of the borough, and its 196-acre grounds provide open space that Wood Green residents treat as their own back garden. The Piccadilly line from Wood Green station puts King's Cross in under twenty minutes and the whole of Central London within straightforward reach, which is useful for daily commuting, but for an airport run with luggage or a pre-dawn departure where the tube is not yet running, a pre-booked affordable taxi in Wood Green is invariably the more sensible option.
