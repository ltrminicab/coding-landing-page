Your Options for Travelling from Gatwick Airport to London Bridge SE1

Travelling from Gatwick Airport to London Bridge SE1 comes with several options. Here is how they compare across comfort, cost, and convenience so you can choose what works best for your journey.


Gatwick Express

The Gatwick Express train service terminates at London Victoria, not London Bridge. Passengers must then take the Underground from Victoria to London Bridge, adding around 15 to 20 minutes of additional travel through a busy station with airport luggage. Total journey time to SE1 with bags is typically 60 to 80 minutes.

Fast to Victoria Tube connection needed

Black Cab

The Gatwick black cab rank is available outside both terminals, but fares are metered and can reach £85 to £125 for the 27-mile run to London Bridge SE1 depending on traffic. There is no fixed price, no flight tracking, and no guaranteed meet and greet inside the arrivals hall.

No pre-booking needed Variable metered fare

Uber

An app-based ride service operates at Gatwick Airport but applies surge pricing during busy flight arrival periods. The price for a Gatwick to London Bridge SE1 run can increase significantly at peak times, and there is no dedicated arrivals hall meet and greet or live flight monitoring included in the standard service.

App-based tracking Surge pricing risk
