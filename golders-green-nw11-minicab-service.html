Discover Golders Green NW11 – North London's Most Distinctive High Street

Golders Green NW11 has developed its own unmistakable character since the Northern line arrived in 1907, transforming what had been open farmland into one of North West London's most recognisable communities. The area has been home to one of Europe's most significant Jewish communities for over a century, and Golders Green Road reflects that heritage with a concentration of kosher restaurants, bakeries, delicatessens, and community businesses that give the high street a quality found nowhere else in London. Golders Hill Park, a free corner of the wider Hampstead Heath estate on the western side of NW11, offers formal gardens, a small zoo, a deer enclosure, and open lawns that remain remarkably quieter than the Heath proper — an underappreciated green space that local residents tend to guard carefully. Golders Green Crematorium on Hoop Lane sits behind striking Lombardic and Byzantine architecture and holds a notable place in cultural history as the site where the ashes of Sigmund Freud, Marc Bolan, and Keith Moon were laid to rest, among many others. The bus terminus beside the station sends routes across North and Central London, making NW11 genuinely well connected for the city at large, and the short distance to Brent Cross Shopping Centre adds a retail convenience that few other north London postcodes can match.
