Discover Forest Gate E7, East London's Green and Connected Community

Forest Gate E7 sits in the London Borough of Newham, bordered by Stratford to the west, Leytonstone to the north, Manor Park to the east, and Upton Park and Plaistow to the south. The postcode takes its name from the former toll gate on the road through Epping Forest and retains a character shaped by Victorian terraced streets, significant open green space, and a strong community identity. Woodgrange Road is the main high street, lined with independent businesses and forming part of the Woodgrange Road Conservation Area, one of several locally protected streetscapes within the postcode. Green Street to the south runs through one of east London's most established market and retail corridors, drawing shoppers from across Newham and beyond.

Wanstead Flats forms the western edge of Epping Forest and covers more than 300 acres of open grassland managed by the City of London Corporation, providing one of the largest areas of open space within east London and a significant natural boundary for the northern part of E7. West Ham Park, situated on the postcode's southern edge, is a formal Victorian park also managed by the City of London Corporation, covering 77 acres and offering sports facilities, ornamental gardens, and open lawns for residents across the surrounding area. Forest Gate and Manor Park stations on the Elizabeth line opened to through-running services in May 2022 and transformed connectivity across E7, providing a direct rail link to the City, the West End, and central stations including Liverpool Street, Bond Street, and Paddington without a change.
