Discover Fulham SW6, South West London's Riverside Neighbourhood

Fulham SW6 sits in the southwest of inner London, bounded by the Thames to the south and west, Chelsea to the east, and Hammersmith to the north. Its character is shaped by Victorian and Edwardian terraced streets, a strong community of long-term residents, and a riverside identity that sets it apart from most other inner-London postcodes of comparable size. The postcode has changed considerably over the past three decades, with Fulham Road and New King's Road both developing into well-regarded dining and shopping destinations, but the residential streets behind them retain the quiet, established feel that makes SW6 one of the most consistently popular addresses in south-west London.

Craven Cottage on Stevenage Road is the defining landmark of the postcode. Fulham FC's riverside ground has stood on its current site since 1896, making it the oldest professional football stadium in London. The Cottage itself, a Victorian pavilion in the corner of the ground, is Grade II* listed, and the stand overlooking the Thames is among the most distinctive football views in the country. With a capacity of around 25,700 following recent redevelopment, Craven Cottage draws significant matchday and event traffic into SW6 from across the city.

Bishops Park and Fulham Palace occupy 36 acres of Thames-side grounds at the southern end of SW6 at Bishops Avenue. The palace served as the official London residence of the Bishops of London for over 1,100 years before being transferred to Hammersmith and Fulham Council. The grounds include a walled kitchen garden, an orchard, formal flower beds, and the site of one of England's longest documented moated enclosures. The combination of the river, the parkland, and the historic buildings makes it one of the most visited green spaces in south-west London.

Parsons Green gives the southern part of the postcode its village feel. The triangular green itself is small, but the surrounding streets of Victorian terraces, independent restaurants, and local pubs create a neighbourhood atmosphere that distinguishes Parsons Green from the busier commercial stretches of Fulham Road to the north. The Hurlingham Club at Ranelagh Gardens adds another layer of south-west London character to SW6, with 42 acres of private grounds on the Thames that have been the home of the club since 1869.
