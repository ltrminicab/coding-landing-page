Discover Woolwich SE18, South-East London's Historic Thames Gateway

Woolwich SE18 occupies a prominent position on the south bank of the Thames in the London Borough of Greenwich, a postcode defined by a remarkable industrial and military heritage stretching back over five centuries. The Royal Arsenal, established in the early 17th century on the eastern side of the town centre, grew to become the largest armaments manufactory in the British Empire, occupying over 1,000 acres of the Woolwich riverfront at its peak during the First World War. The site now anchors a major regeneration project combining residential development, the Crossrail Place retail quarter, and the Royal Arsenal Riverside district on the Thames, which has brought significant new population and investment to SE18 over the past decade.

Woolwich Arsenal station became one of the most significant transport nodes in south-east London in 2022 when the Elizabeth line (Crossrail) extended its services to include a dedicated underground station beneath the Arsenal complex, providing high-frequency connections to Canary Wharf, the City, the West End, Paddington, and onward to Reading and Heathrow. The station also serves National Rail and DLR connections from the existing surface-level Woolwich Arsenal station, making the interchange one of the most connected in the outer east of London. The historic Woolwich foot tunnel, opened in 1912, continues to provide pedestrian access under the Thames to North Woolwich on the Essex side.

  • Royal Arsenal – a 500-year-old armaments complex on the Thames waterfront, now transformed into a major mixed-use development combining residential quarters, retail, the Royal Arsenal Riverside district, and the Firepower Royal Artillery Museum
  • Woolwich Ferry – the free pedestrian and vehicle ferry crossing the Thames between Woolwich and North Woolwich, operating since 1889 and one of only two free ferry crossings on the tidal Thames in Greater London
  • Woolwich Foot Tunnel – the 502-metre pedestrian tunnel under the Thames opened in 1912, connecting SE18 to the north side of the river and providing an alternative crossing alongside the ferry
  • Woolwich Common – a large area of common land in the southern part of SE18, surrounded by the Royal Military Academy (now residential) and providing open green space in an area otherwise defined by its industrial and urban character
