Discover Highbury N5 – Highbury Fields, Victorian Terraces, and North London Village Life

Highbury N5 is one of inner north London's most consistently sought-after residential postcodes, centred on the broad green expanse of Highbury Fields and the wide, tree-lined streets that radiate outward through the London Borough of Islington. The area is characterised by some of the finest Victorian and Edwardian terrace housing in north London, attracting families and professionals who prize architectural quality, proximity to green space, and the convenience of two stations — Highbury & Islington on the Victoria line and London Overground — within easy walking distance. The Victoria line provides a direct link southward to King's Cross, Oxford Circus, and Victoria in under ten minutes, making the postcode highly attractive to frequent business travellers with airport commitments.

Highbury Fields itself is one of the largest open spaces in inner London, covering 29 acres with tennis courts, a lido, a children's play area, and open lawns used year-round by local residents. The fields adjoin Highbury Grove and Highbury Hill, two of the postcode's most desirable residential streets, where double-fronted Victorian villas and generous garden terraces command a strong premium. The Emirates Stadium, home of Arsenal Football Club, sits on Avenell Road at the eastern edge of N5, bringing international visitors and matchday crowds to the neighbourhood on a regular basis and driving strong demand for reliable private transfer services on event days.

The postcode is also home to Highbury Corner, the busy roundabout junction at its southern end where the A1 meets Upper Street and the Holloway Road, providing fast road access southbound toward Angel and the City. Avenell Road, Calabria Road, Aubert Park, and the quiet residential streets around Mountgrove Road give Highbury N5 a layered residential character ranging from family-focused terraces to sought-after garden flats.
