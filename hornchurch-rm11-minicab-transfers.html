Discover Hornchurch RM11 – A Historic Havering Suburb and the Home of the Battle of Britain's Most Famous Airfield

Hornchurch RM11 is a prosperous and well-established residential suburb in the London Borough of Havering, occupying the southeastern quadrant of Greater London approximately 15 miles east of central London and bordered by Upminster to the east, Emerson Park to the north, Elm Park to the west, and Rainham to the south toward the Thames. The postcode covers the compact town centre on the High Street and Station Lane, the leafy Emerson Park conservation area with its distinctive detached Edwardian and inter-war villas, and the open green space of Hornchurch Country Park on the southern edge of the postcode — a 55-hectare parkland managed by the London Borough of Havering on the site of the former RAF Hornchurch aerodrome, one of the most operationally significant fighter stations in the history of the Royal Air Force. RAF Hornchurch was a front-line Spitfire base throughout the Battle of Britain in 1940, flying from this east London location to engage Luftwaffe formations over the Thames Estuary and Kent coast, and the airfield's role in that critical period of the war is commemorated at the Country Park through heritage information boards, preserved revetments, and the wider landscape of the former aerodrome perimeter.

Hornchurch station on the District line provides Underground services westbound into central London and the City, while Romford and Harold Wood stations nearby offer Elizabeth line connections and National Rail services. For Heathrow travel the most practical route is the A124 west to the A13 and then the A406 North Circular westbound to the A40 — a predominantly A-road cross-London route that our drivers plan in real time using live traffic data to minimise journey time from your RM11 door to the terminal.
