Discover Potters Bar EN6 – A South Hertfordshire Commuter Town on the M25

Potters Bar EN6 is a well-established and largely prosperous commuter town in the Hertsmere district of south Hertfordshire, occupying a broad elevated plateau between the M25 to the south and the gently rolling farmland of the Hertfordshire countryside to the north. The postcode spans from the town centre along the High Street and Darkes Lane corridor northward through the residential streets of Oakmere, Cranborne, and Little Heath toward the Brookmans Park boundary, taking in a predominantly mid-twentieth century residential landscape of semis and detached houses on wide suburban streets interspersed with golf courses, playing fields, and the mature green corridors of the Gobions and Wrotham Park estate woodland. Potters Bar's identity as a commuter settlement was firmly established by the arrival of the Great Northern Railway in the nineteenth century and subsequently reinforced by the construction of the M25, which passes through the southern edge of the postcode at Junction 24 and gives residents one of the most convenient motorway on-ramp locations of any Hertfordshire town.
