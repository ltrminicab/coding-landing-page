Discover Hendon NW4 – Home of the RAF Museum and the Welsh Harp

Hendon NW4 sits in the north of the London Borough of Barnet, an area that built its modern character around the Northern line extension that arrived in the 1920s and has continued to develop steadily ever since. The RAF Museum on Grahame Park Way is the postcode's most celebrated landmark, housing more than a hundred aircraft from across aviation history — including Spitfires, Hurricanes, and Lancaster bombers — in a collection that is entirely free to enter and stands alongside the best aviation museums anywhere in the world. The Welsh Harp, formally known as Brent Reservoir, forms a remarkable contrast just to the southwest: 400 acres of open water and wetland designated as a Site of Special Scientific Interest, where sailing clubs, open-water swimmers, and nesting birds share one of the largest stretches of freshwater in North London. Church Farmhouse Museum in Greyhound Hill occupies one of Hendon's oldest surviving buildings, a Grade II* listed farmhouse from the 1660s that now holds local history exhibitions and community events. Brent Cross Shopping Centre is within easy reach to the south, and the mix of Jewish, South Asian, and other communities along and around Hendon Central gives the high street an unpretentious variety that has been part of this postcode's character for decades.
