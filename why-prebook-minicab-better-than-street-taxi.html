Understanding the Two Options

Before getting into the detail, it helps to be clear about what each service actually is and how the two systems work in practice.

A street taxi, often called a hackney carriage, is a licensed vehicle that is legally permitted to pick up passengers who hail them from the street or find them waiting at a taxi rank. These vehicles operate with a meter that runs from the moment you get in. The fare at the end of your journey depends on distance, time, and the rate your driver applies, which can vary depending on the hour and the day of the week.

A minicab is a licensed private hire vehicle that must be booked in advance through an operator. You cannot hail a minicab from the street. Instead, you contact the company through a website, an app, or a phone call, and a driver is assigned to you at a confirmed time and location. The fare is agreed before the journey begins, not after it ends.

These two options serve the same basic purpose, but the experience they deliver and the value they offer are quite different in almost every meaningful way.

1. Price Certainty: Know Your Fare Before You Travel

This is the single most consistent reason people give when asked why they prefer prebooked minicabs over street taxis. With a minicab, you receive a confirmed fare at the time of booking. Whether the traffic is moving freely or sitting completely still on the motorway, the price you agreed to is the price you pay.

With a metered street taxi, the fare is always uncertain. A 30 minute journey in good conditions might cost one amount, and the same journey in rush hour traffic could cost significantly more. There is no way to know in advance, and by the time you arrive at your destination, you are committed to whatever the meter reads.

Real world example: A journey from Heathrow Airport to a Central London hotel in normal traffic might cost around £55 in a metered taxi. In heavy traffic during peak hours, the same journey can exceed £90. A prebooked minicab for the same route is typically confirmed at a fixed price of around £45 to £60, regardless of traffic conditions on the day.

For anyone travelling to an airport, attending a business meeting, or working to a budget, this unpredictability is a genuine problem. Prebooking removes it entirely and puts you fully in control of your transport costs.

3. Your Safety Is Not Left to Chance

When you step into a prebooked minicab, you know exactly who is driving. You received their name, their vehicle registration, and in many cases a photo before they ever arrived at your location. Safety tip: Always verify the driver's name, vehicle registration, and company name against your booking confirmation before entering any vehicle. A legitimate prebooked driver will confirm your name and destination without you having to volunteer that information first.

7. Consistent Quality Every Single Time

When Is a Street Taxi Still Useful?

To be completely balanced, there are situations where a street taxi makes genuine sense. If you are already in a busy city centre with no phone battery and no internet access, flagging down a licensed taxi from a properly marked rank is a perfectly reasonable solution. If you need to move immediately and have absolutely no ability to book in advance, a taxi rank offers an instant option.

For truly spontaneous, immediate travel within a busy city centre area where licensed taxis are readily available, the street taxi still has a place. The point is simply that for the great majority of journeys, and especially any journey that involves airports, hotels, early mornings, groups, or any kind of planning at all, prebooking a minicab is consistently the smarter, safer, and more cost effective choice.

Tips for Getting the Most from Your Prebooked Minicab

  1. Book as early as possible. The more notice you give, the more choice you have over vehicle type and departure time. For airport runs, booking at least the night before is strongly recommended.
  2. Be precise about passenger numbers and luggage. Providing accurate information at the time of booking means the right vehicle arrives. Surprising your driver with excess luggage on the day creates unnecessary problems.
  3. Provide your correct flight number. Your driver uses this to track your arrival and adjust the pickup time automatically if your service is delayed. If your flight number changes due to a rebooking, update your booking as soon as possible.
  4. Confirm the pickup point in advance. Most hotels have a clear main entrance, but in busy central locations there may be restrictions on vehicle access at certain times. Confirming the exact meeting point before the day removes any last minute confusion.
  5. Save your driver's contact number on your phone. On the day of travel, having a direct line to your driver is reassuring and practical. If anything changes on your end, a quick message keeps everything on track.
  6. Check your booking confirmation details carefully. Make sure the date, time, pickup address, destination, and vehicle type all match your requirements before the day arrives.
  7. Verify the driver before entering the vehicle. Match the driver's name and vehicle registration to your confirmation. A legitimate driver will confirm your name and destination without you volunteering that information first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is prebooking a minicab always cheaper than a street taxi?

In most cases, yes. The fixed fare structure of a prebooked minicab removes the risk of a metered fare inflating due to traffic, diversions, or peak hour surcharges. For airport runs, longer journeys, and travel during busy periods, the difference in total cost can be significant. For very short journeys in light central London traffic, the difference is smaller, but the certainty of a fixed price still represents better value for most passengers.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

When you prebook a minicab for an airport arrival, your driver tracks your flight in real time as a standard part of the service. If your flight lands an hour later than scheduled, your driver is aware and adjusts their arrival accordingly. You are not charged extra for the delay, and you do not need to contact the company unless your plans change entirely.

Can I cancel a prebooked minicab if my plans change?

Yes. With LTR Minicab, free cancellation is available up to 12 hours before your journey. This gives you meaningful flexibility without financial penalty and makes prebooking a low risk decision even when your plans are not entirely fixed at the time of booking.

Can I book a minicab for a group of eight people?

Yes. Prebooked minicab services offer a full range of vehicle sizes designed for groups. An MPV 8 or executive MPV can accommodate groups of up to eight passengers along with their luggage, all travelling in the same vehicle rather than splitting across multiple separate cars.

Is a prebooked minicab safe to use at night?

Yes, and in many respects it is safer than hailing an unknown vehicle late at night. With a prebooked minicab, you know the driver's name, vehicle registration, and the company they work for before they arrive. Do I need to tip a prebooked minicab driver?

Tipping is entirely optional and at your discretion. The fixed fare you agreed at the time of booking covers the full cost of your journey including fuel, waiting time within the agreed allowance, and luggage assistance. If you receive excellent service and wish to show appreciation, any gratuity is always welcome but never expected or required.

Yes. LTR Minicab operates 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. For very early morning departures such as those at 4am or 5am, booking in advance is especially important because street taxi availability at those hours is unpredictable. What should I do if the vehicle that arrives does not match my booking?

This is one of the key reasons why saving your driver's contact details and verifying the vehicle before entering is such an important habit to build.
