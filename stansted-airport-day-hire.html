Built-In Hourly Waiting Allowance

We understand that business meetings, lunches, and flight connections rarely run exactly to the minute. Every Stansted day hire package includes 20 minutes of complimentary waiting time for every hour of your booking. Book an 8-hour day hire and you accumulate a rolling buffer of 160 minutes of standby waiting time across the day — your driver will not start charging immediate overtime the second a meeting overruns.

For your initial Stansted pickup, the meter does not start until your wheels are down — your day hire includes a 60-minute complimentary grace period from landing to account for customs and baggage reclaim, after which the 20-minutes-per-hour standby rule applies for every later stop.

What's Included in Your Stansted Day Hire

A Stansted Airport day hire from LTR Minicab is built around flexibility, transparency, and comfort. Whatever your itinerary, your day is covered with no hidden extras.

Chauffeur Standby & Recall Protocols

  • While you are in a meeting, lunch, or visiting an attraction, your driver remains on call within a 3 to 5-minute radius.
  • Simply text or call your chauffeur and they will pull up outside the venue as you step out.
  • Extended waiting beyond your allowance is billed transparently at £10 per 15-minute block, or part thereof.
  • A full extended hour is billed at the standard hourly extension rate, which automatically includes another 20 minutes of waiting capability.
  • Itemised time and mileage logs can be provided at the end of the day on request.
  • On-board Wi-Fi, phone charging, and bottled water available on request for executive and MPV bookings.
  • Child seats can be arranged for family day hire bookings from Stansted.

Stansted Day Hire — Fees, Tolls, and Driver Hours Explained

To keep your Stansted Airport day hire completely transparent, here is everything covered in your quote and what may apply separately depending on your route.

  • London Congestion Charge: £18 applies for journeys into central London on weekdays 7am–6pm and weekends/bank holidays 12pm–6pm. This is confirmed in your quote if your itinerary enters the zone.
  • ULEZ Charge: Our vehicles meet ULEZ emission standards, so this £12.50 charge does not apply to LTR Minicab day hire bookings.
  • Dartford Crossing: If your itinerary heads toward Kent or Gatwick via the Dartford Crossing, the toll is added to your final quote.
  • Out-of-Hours Premium: Day hires starting before 6am or running past 11pm may attract a standard night-rate premium on the base hourly rate.
  • Driver Hours & Two-Driver Days: UK driving regulations limit a single chauffeur to a maximum of 10 hours driving per day. For itineraries spanning more than 10–12 hours, we arrange a driver swap or a second chauffeur, which is reflected in your quote.
  • Vehicle Breakdown Cover: In the unlikely event of a breakdown, a like-for-like replacement vehicle is dispatched immediately to continue your day.
  • Gratuity: Tipping is optional and entirely at your discretion — it is never added automatically to your invoice unless requested.
