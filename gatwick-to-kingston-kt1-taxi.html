Weighing Up Your Options for Getting from Gatwick Airport to Kingston KT1

Passengers travelling between Gatwick and Kingston upon Thames have a number of options. Here is how each one compares so you can make an informed choice based on your schedule, group size, and luggage.


Train via Waterloo

Train travel from Gatwick to Kingston requires a Thameslink or Southern service into London Waterloo or London Bridge, followed by a South Western Railway connection out to Kingston station. The full journey with interchange takes around 75 to 100 minutes and involves managing airport luggage across multiple platforms and concourses.

Rail option Two trains required

Black Cab

Black cabs are available at the Gatwick taxi rank and can travel directly to Kingston KT1 on the meter. The 27 to 30-mile journey typically costs between £80 and £120 depending on traffic, with no price confirmed before you board, no flight tracking, and no arrivals name board included.

Direct journey No fixed price

Uber

Uber operates at Gatwick but fares surge considerably during peak arrival times on busy weekday mornings and at weekends. There is no guaranteed flight monitoring, no name board in arrivals, and the price estimate shown before requesting a ride may increase once a driver accepts during high-demand periods.
