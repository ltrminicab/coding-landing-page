The E14 postcode covers one of London's most remarkable and varied districts, encompassing the whole of the Isle of Dogs peninsula together with the surrounding docklands areas of Poplar, Blackwall, Limehouse, and East India Dock on the north bank of the Thames in the London Borough of Tower Hamlets. The towers of One Canada Square, Citigroup Centre, HSBC Tower, and the more recently completed cluster around Crossrail Place and Wood Wharf dominate the northern skyline of E14 and house tens of thousands of workers in banking, finance, law, and professional services. Below the Canary Wharf development the island opens into the broader residential communities that make up the older fabric of the Isle of Dogs. Manchester Road is the main north-south artery running through the centre of the peninsula from the Westferry DLR station to the tip of the island at Island Gardens, where the foot tunnel leads under the Thames to Greenwich on the south bank. Mudchute Park and Farm lies between Cubitt Town and Millwall and is one of the largest urban farms in London, occupying a broad stretch of open grassland in the middle of the island that gives E14 a surprisingly rural feel in the heart of the docklands. Moving north from the island proper, the E14 postcode extends across the neck of land connecting the peninsula to the mainland, taking in the area around Marsh Wall and Millharbour at the western edge, Prestons Road and the northern end of the island, and then continuing north into Blackwall, East India, and Poplar, where older terraced streets and newer developments sit alongside the DLR lines that provide the island's main public transport connections.
