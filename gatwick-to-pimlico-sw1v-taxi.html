The SW1V postcode covers a characterful and densely residential stretch of inner central London sitting just south of Victoria station and east of Belgravia, encompassing the entirety of Pimlico as one of Westminster's most distinct historic neighbourhoods. The postcode takes in the formal garden squares at its heart, beginning with Warwick Square and its large private central garden surrounded by elegant stucco townhouses, Eccleston Square a little to the north offering a similarly grand arrangement of terraced housing around a well-kept private garden used by residents, and St George's Square running down to the Thames Embankment and Grosvenor Road where the riverfront opens up towards Vauxhall Bridge. Tachbrook Street runs north from Lupus Street as the neighbourhood's principal shopping and café street, meeting the Vauxhall Bridge Road at the north east corner where the postcode boundary meets Victoria. The northern edge of SW1V is formed by Gillingham Street and Ebury Bridge Road as they approach Victoria station, while the western boundary follows the line of Belgrave Road where the postcode meets Belgravia and SW1W.
