Liverpool Street EC2M – The City of London's Eastern Financial Hub and Major Transport Gateway

Liverpool Street EC2M is one of the most significant postcodes in the City of London, covering the area immediately surrounding Liverpool Street station — the third busiest mainline terminus in the United Kingdom — and extending across the Broadgate estate, one of Europe's largest purpose-built office campuses, which houses the European headquarters of major global investment banks, law firms, and professional services companies within the immediate EC2M footprint. The station itself serves as the main London terminus for Greater Anglia services into East Anglia, Essex, Hertfordshire, and the Stansted Express to Stansted Airport, while the Underground complex beneath provides access to the Central, Circle, Hammersmith and City, and Metropolitan lines, as well as the Elizabeth line which opened in 2022 and connects Liverpool Street directly westbound to Heathrow Airport and eastbound to Shenfield and Abbey Wood in a single direct service for the first time in the station's history.

The EC2M postcode also incorporates the western reaches of Bishopsgate and the streets running north from Bank junction through Threadneedle Street into the Moorgate and Finsbury Square corridor, placing it at the intersection of the City's historical banking core and its modern office and technology district. The area is consistently among the highest in demand for pre-booked executive transfers arriving from UK airports, with Gatwick Airport being a primary origin for business travellers arriving into the City from European short-haul routes, making the Gatwick to Liverpool Street EC2M taxi one of the most frequently requested City airport transfer routes in the LTR Minicab network.

Which Gatwick to Liverpool Street Travel Option is Right for You?

Travelling from Gatwick Airport to Liverpool Street EC2M comes with several options. Here is how they compare across comfort, cost, and convenience so you can choose what works best for your journey.


Gatwick Express

The Gatwick Express runs to London Victoria, not Liverpool Street. Passengers must then take the Circle or District line from Victoria to Liverpool Street, adding a 20-minute Underground connection with airport luggage across a busy interchange. Total journey time with bags is typically 65 to 90 minutes.

Fast to Victoria Change required for EC2M

Black Cab

London black cabs are available at Gatwick's taxi ranks but operate on a metered fare. The 29-mile journey to Liverpool Street EC2M can cost £90–£130 depending on traffic, with no price certainty before you set off and no flight-tracking or meet and greet service.

No pre-booking needed Unpredictable fare

Uber

Uber operates at Gatwick but is subject to surge pricing during busy arrival periods, particularly on early morning flights and bank holidays. Prices for the Gatwick to Liverpool Street EC2M run can increase significantly without notice, and there is no dedicated meet and greet or flight monitoring included.
