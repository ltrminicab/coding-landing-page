Discover Ruislip HA4 – Ruislip Lido, Manor Farm, and London's Western Woodland Gateway

Ruislip HA4 is a leafy residential postcode in the London Borough of Hillingdon, positioned in the far west of Greater London approximately seven miles northeast of Heathrow Airport. The postcode encompasses the historic village core of Ruislip around Manor Farm and the High Street, the residential areas of Ruislip Manor to the south and Eastcote to the east, and extends northward toward the woodland boundary of Ruislip Woods National Nature Reserve. Despite being within the Greater London boundary, HA4 retains a strongly semi-rural character shaped by its mature tree cover, Edwardian and interwar housing stock, and proximity to the Metropolitan Green Belt. Ruislip station and Ruislip Manor station on the Metropolitan and Piccadilly lines provide Underground connections into central London, while West Ruislip station at the western terminus of the Central line gives residents a further option for east-westbound Tube travel.

Ruislip Lido is the postcode's most distinctive local landmark — a large open-air reservoir converted into a freshwater beach and leisure park, operated by the London Borough of Hillingdon and one of the few genuine sandy beach environments within Greater London. The adjacent Ruislip Woods, covering over 300 acres of ancient woodland, are designated a National Nature Reserve and provide extensive walking and cycling routes used by residents across HA4 and the wider Hillingdon area. Manor Farm, located on the edge of the Ruislip village centre, preserves a 12th century tithe barn, a medieval moat, and a local history museum that make the area one of the most historically significant residential postcodes in west London. The High Street village core retains an independent retail character with cafes, restaurants, and boutiques that distinguish Ruislip from larger surrounding commercial centres.

Ruislip HA4's proximity to Heathrow Airport — approximately seven miles via the A4180 and A4020 — makes the postcode one of the most conveniently situated in Greater London for airport access, with journey times of 20 to 30 minutes achievable in normal conditions. LTR Minicab covers every HA4 address with fixed-price airport transfers, a full vehicle fleet, and licensed drivers available throughout the day and night, providing Ruislip residents with a door-to-door service that makes the short Heathrow run both simple and price-certain.
