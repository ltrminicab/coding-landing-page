Discover Rickmansworth WD3 – A Colne Valley Market Town on the Edge of the Chilterns

Rickmansworth WD3 is a highly regarded and scenically situated market town in the Three Rivers district of Hertfordshire, positioned at the confluence of three rivers — the Chess, the Gade, and the Colne — at the southern edge of the Chiltern Hills Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty. The postcode covers a wide and varied area from the town centre and Aquadrome recreational park in the south through the residential neighbourhoods of Chorleywood, Croxley Green, and Maple Cross to the north, and the wooded villages of Sarratt and Chenies to the northwest, taking in a landscape of traditional market town streets, private-road estates such as Moor Park, and extensive Areas of Special Landscape Character along the Chess and Colne valleys. Rickmansworth has maintained its identity as a distinct Hertfordshire market settlement rather than a commuter suburb of London, with a town centre centred on the High Street and Church Street retaining independent shops, restaurants, and a weekly market alongside the historic St Mary the Virgin parish church.

Rickmansworth station on the Metropolitan line provides Zone 7 Underground services direct into Baker Street, King's Cross St Pancras, and the City via the Aldgate branch, while Moor Park and Chorleywood stations also within WD3 offer additional boarding points on the same line for residents in the northern and western parts of the postcode. The M25 at Junction 17 (Maple Cross) is accessible in under five minutes by road from the town centre, placing WD3 among the best-connected Hertfordshire postcodes for motorway access to both Heathrow and the wider M25 orbital network.
