Discover West Kensington W14 – A Vibrant Gateway Between Kensington and Hammersmith

West Kensington W14 is a dynamic and well-connected west London neighbourhood positioned between the grand residential streets of Kensington to the north and the commercial heart of Hammersmith to the west. The area is best known for Olympia London, one of the capital's most prestigious exhibition and events venues, which draws hundreds of thousands of visitors each year for trade shows, consumer fairs, and major conferences. West Kensington benefits from excellent underground connections via the District line at both West Kensington and Barons Court stations, as well as Kensington (Olympia) station on the Overground, placing the rest of London within effortless reach. The neighbourhood itself is characterised by handsome Victorian terraces, a thriving high street along North End Road, and a community feel that makes it a popular choice for both long-term residents and business travellers seeking a well-located base in west London.
