Where to Meet Your LTR Minicab Driver at Heathrow

Terminal 2 (The Queen's Terminal): Your driver will meet you inside the Terminal 2 arrivals hall on the ground floor, holding a personalised name board near the designated meeting point beyond the customs exit.

Terminal 3: Meet your driver in the Terminal 3 arrivals hall ground floor, past the baggage reclaim and customs area. Your driver will be clearly visible with a name board at the central arrivals meeting area.

Terminal 4: Your driver will meet you in the Terminal 4 arrivals hall. Terminal 4 is accessed via the free Heathrow Express shuttle from Terminals 2 and 3 — your driver will confirm the exact meeting point by message on landing.

Terminal 5 (British Airways): Meet your driver in the Terminal 5 arrivals hall on the ground floor, directly past the customs exit. The T5 dedicated private hire vehicle zone is signposted from the arrivals concourse — your driver will guide you via WhatsApp or SMS.

For hotel and residential pickups going to Heathrow, your driver will arrive at your confirmed address and wait in the designated pickup area. All Heathrow taxi bookings include free waiting on departures and real-time flight monitoring on arrivals, so your journey runs smoothly regardless of delays or early landings.
