Discover Islington N1 – Angel, Upper Street, and the Heart of Inner North London

Islington N1 is one of inner London's most distinctive and culturally rich postcodes, encompassing the Angel neighbourhood, Upper Street, Barnsbury, and Canonbury in the London Borough of Islington. The area is renowned for its mix of Georgian and Victorian terraced streets, a thriving independent restaurant and bar scene centred on Upper Street, and a concentration of creative and media industry workers drawn by proximity to the City, King's Cross, and the tech clusters of Shoreditch and Old Street. Angel station on the Northern line provides fast underground connections southward to the City and West End and northward through the wider London network.

Islington has a strong performing arts tradition anchored by Sadler's Wells Theatre on Rosebery Avenue, one of the UK's premier dance venues, and the Almeida Theatre on Almeida Street, which consistently produces critically acclaimed productions. Camden Passage, a narrow pedestrian alley running off Upper Street near Angel, is a long-established antiques and vintage market that draws collectors and browsers from across London and internationally. Regent's Canal runs through the northern edge of N1, providing a scenic towpath walk connecting Islington through to King's Cross and eastward to Victoria Park.

The postcode's central position gives residents straightforward road access westbound via the A501 Marylebone Road corridor toward the A40 and Heathrow, as well as easy connections to the M1 northbound and the wider motorway network. King's Cross and St Pancras International stations are both within minutes of the N1 boundary, offering international rail services and domestic high-speed connections.
