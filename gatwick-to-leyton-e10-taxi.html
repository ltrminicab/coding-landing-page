The E10 postcode covers Leyton, bordered by Walthamstow E17 to the north, Leytonstone E11 to the east, Stratford and the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park to the south, and the River Lea and Hackney Marshes to the west. Leyton station on the Central line and Leyton Midland Road on the Overground both provide quick connections into central London and toward Stratford.
