Discover Stanmore HA7 – An Elevated Village on London's Northern Ridge

Stanmore HA7 is a prestigious and leafy residential postcode in the London Borough of Harrow, occupying the elevated ridge of Stanmore Hill at the very northern perimeter of Greater London where the built-up suburbs give way to Hertfordshire countryside. The postcode spans from the Jubilee line terminus at Stanmore station in the south through the mature residential streets of Old Church Lane, The Broadway, and Dennis Lane toward the Harrow Weald boundary to the east and the Elstree and Borehamwood border to the north, taking in a varied landscape of large detached houses on private roads, Edwardian semis near the village centre, and gated developments along the wooded lanes of Great Stanmore parish. Stanmore is shaped by its historic identity as a distinct village predating London's twentieth-century expansion, and the area retains a self-contained character reinforced by the presence of Bentley Priory Museum — housed in the former RAF Fighter Command headquarters where the Battle of Britain was directed — Stanmore Country Park, and the ancient woodland of Stanmore Common bordering the Hertfordshire boundary.

Stanmore station at the southern end of HA7 serves as the western terminus of the Jubilee line, providing Zone 5 Underground services through Wembley Park, Canary Wharf, and the Docklands corridor, while Canons Park station on the same line sits just south of the postcode boundary providing an additional boarding point.
