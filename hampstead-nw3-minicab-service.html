Discover Hampstead NW3 – London's Literary Village Above the City

Few parts of London have held onto their character quite the way Hampstead has. The village centre around Flask Walk and Heath Street still feels genuinely separate from the rest of the capital, with Georgian townhouses, independent coffee shops, and pubs that have been here for centuries sitting comfortably alongside the area's reputation for culture and quiet wealth. Hampstead Heath stretches across 791 acres of ancient woodland, meadows, and hills, with Parliament Hill offering one of the most striking open views across London's skyline and the bathing ponds providing a swimming experience that is entirely unique in the city. Kenwood House on the northern edge of the Heath holds a remarkable free art collection including works by Rembrandt and Vermeer, and its lakeside concert series draws audiences throughout the summer. The Freud Museum on Maresfield Gardens preserves the final home of Sigmund Freud, while Keats House on Keats Grove is where the poet wrote Ode to a Nightingale and several of his best-known works. The Spaniards Inn on the Heath's edge has been serving visitors since the sixteenth century, and the neighbourhood's literary roll call — Keats, Lawrence, Aldous Huxley, Kingsley Amis — gives NW3 a cultural weight that very few postcodes can match.
