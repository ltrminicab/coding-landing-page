The E2 postcode covers Bethnal Green and parts of Shoreditch, bordered by Hackney E8 to the north, Stepney and Mile End E1 to the south and east, and the City of London and Shoreditch EC postcodes to the west. The main spine of the postcode runs along Bethnal Green Road, a busy commercial street lined with shops, cafes, and markets, connecting through to Shoreditch in the west and Cambridge Heath Road in the east. To the north sits Columbia Road, famous for its Sunday flower market and rows of independent shops housed in Victorian shopfronts, with Hackney Road running parallel and forming part of the boundary with Hackney E8. Brick Lane forms the western edge of E2, well known for its curry houses, vintage shops, and street art, and connecting through to Shoreditch High Street and the City. To the east, Cambridge Heath Road leads up toward London Fields and Hackney, passing close to the Museum of Childhood and Bethnal Green Underground and Overground stations. South of Bethnal Green Road, streets including Pollard Row, Wellington Row, Three Colts Lane, and Patriot Square form a dense network of period terraces and council estates, many built around small parks and squares. Further south and east, Globe Road and Roman Road run toward Mile End, taking in Victoria Park's western edge, one of east London's largest and most popular green spaces, with its boating lake, café, and extensive paths along the Hertford Union Canal. The Regent's Canal also passes through the northern part of E2, with towpath access connecting through to Hackney and Islington. Bethnal Green E2 combines historic markets, converted warehouses, council estates, and new residential developments, and LTR Minicab drivers are familiar with every street and turning across the postcode to bring you directly to your door.
