Gatwick Airport to Bow E3 Taxi Transfer

The E3 postcode covers Bow and parts of Bromley-by-Bow, bordered by Victoria Park and Hackney Wick to the north, Mile End and Stepney E1 to the south and west, and Stratford E15 to the east. The main thoroughfare, Bow Road, runs through the heart of the postcode, lined with shops, takeaways, and bus routes connecting toward the City and Stratford. Roman Road forms a key shopping street to the north, home to a long-running street market and a growing number of independent cafes and boutiques, with Grove Road and St Stephen's Road running close by toward Victoria Park's eastern entrances. To the east, Fish Island sits between the Hertford Union Canal and the River Lea, an area of converted warehouses, artist studios, and new apartment blocks close to the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park and Here East. Bow Quarter, a converted match factory turned residential complex, lies just off Fairfield Road, while Tredegar Square and the surrounding streets offer some of the postcode's best preserved Victorian terraces. Further south, Bow Church and Bow Road stations sit close to the A11 and A12 junctions, with Mile End Park stretching along the Regent's Canal toward Mile End. Bromley-by-Bow, in the eastern part of E3, includes Three Mills Island and the approach to the A102 and Blackwall Tunnel. Bow E3 blends historic terraces, industrial conversions, and waterside developments, and LTR Minicab drivers know every street, square, and turning across the postcode to bring you directly to your door.
