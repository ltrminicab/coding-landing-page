Discover Angel N1 – Upper Street, Georgian Terraces, and the Heart of Islington

Angel N1 is one of the most distinctive and energetic neighbourhoods in inner north London, anchored by the junction of Upper Street, City Road, and Pentonville Road at Angel station in the London Borough of Islington. The area is defined by its blend of Georgian terraced streets, a vibrant independent restaurant and bar culture stretching the length of Upper Street, and a concentration of creative professionals and long-established residents who value both community character and fast connections into the City and West End. Angel station on the Northern line is one of London's busiest interchanges, providing a direct and rapid link to Bank, London Bridge, and beyond.

Camden Passage, a narrow pedestrian alley running just off Upper Street near the station, is one of London's most celebrated antiques and vintage markets, lined with specialist dealers, covered arcades, and weekend stalls drawing collectors and browsers from across the capital. Chapel Market, running parallel to Liverpool Road, is one of the last surviving traditional street markets in inner London, open six days a week with fruit, vegetables, and general goods. The Business Design Centre on Upper Street occupies a Victorian exhibition hall and hosts major trade shows, exhibitions, and conferences throughout the year, bringing a consistent flow of business visitors into the N1 area.

Sadler's Wells Theatre on Rosebery Avenue and the Almeida Theatre on Almeida Street both sit within easy walking distance of Angel station, giving the neighbourhood a strong performing arts identity that draws theatre and dance audiences on evenings and weekends. Regent's Canal is accessible from the northern edge of N1 via the towpath near King's Cross, and Colebrooke Row — a quiet, tree-lined street running parallel to City Road — is regarded as one of the most attractive residential addresses in inner London.
