Discover Olympic Park E20 – East London's Legacy of Sporting Excellence and Urban Renewal

Olympic Park E20 occupies one of the most dramatically transformed areas in the entire United Kingdom — a former industrial wasteland in east London's Lower Lea Valley that was radically redeveloped to host the London 2012 Olympic and Paralympic Games, and has since evolved into one of the capital's most exciting and fastest-growing neighbourhoods. The Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, which sits at the heart of E20, stretches across 560 acres of landscaped parkland, waterways, and world-class sporting infrastructure, drawing millions of visitors every year from across London, the UK, and internationally. The iconic London Stadium, now home to West Ham United Football Club and a premier venue for major concerts and global athletics events, dominates the southern end of the park, while the striking London Aquatics Centre — designed by the late architect Dame Zaha Hadid in her signature fluid, wave-like style — continues to serve as both a public swimming facility and a landmark of contemporary design. The Velodrome, praised by cycling bodies worldwide for its exceptional technical specification, and the ArcelorMittal Orbit — the UK's tallest sculpture offering panoramic views across London from its observation deck and the world's longest tunnel slide — complete the park's extraordinary collection of architectural landmarks. Beyond the sporting venues, the park's legacy development has created an entirely new neighbourhood: the East Village, the former Athletes' Village converted into nearly 2,800 residential units, is one of the most comprehensively planned new communities in London's recent history. Westfield Stratford City, one of the largest urban shopping centres in Europe, sits adjacent to Stratford International and Stratford stations, providing access to more than 300 shops, restaurants, and a cinema complex. Here East, the former Olympic Media Centre and Broadcast Centre converted into a pioneering technology and creative campus, now hosts BT Sport, Loughborough University London, and dozens of innovative start-up and scale-up businesses, reinforcing E20's reputation as a major hub for east London's growing digital economy.
