Angel sits at the southern tip of the N1 postcode and forms one of north London's busiest crossroads, where Upper Street, City Road, Pentonville Road, and St John Street all converge around the historic Angel junction. The area takes its name from the old Angel Inn coaching house and today is known for its shopping centre, cinema, and the constant flow of people moving between Islington and the City. Heading north along Upper Street from the junction takes you toward Islington Green and Camden Passage, while City Road runs south east toward Old Street and the tech cluster around Silicon Roundabout. St John Street threads south from Angel down through Clerkenwell, lined with converted warehouses and independent cafes, and Goswell Road runs parallel a little further east connecting toward the Barbican. Pentonville Road climbs west from Angel toward King's Cross, passing the terraces around Crinan Street and the approach to the Regent's Canal towpath, which loops behind Angel offering a quieter walking route alongside the moored narrowboats. Chapel Market, just off Liverpool Road, remains one of the area's best known street markets, drawing shoppers throughout the week for fresh produce and household goods. The residential streets behind Liverpool Road and around Gibson Square feature handsome Georgian terraces typical of the wider Islington borough, while newer developments near the canal and around the Angel Central complex provide modern apartment living close to the Underground station. LTR Minicab drivers know every junction, side street, and one-way restriction around Angel and take you straight to your destination without delay.
