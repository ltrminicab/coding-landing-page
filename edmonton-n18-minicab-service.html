Discover Edmonton N18 – A North London Area with a Quiet History

Edmonton N18 sits in the northern reaches of the London Borough of Enfield, and while it rarely makes the tourist guides, it carries more history than most people realise. The Romantic poet Charles Lamb spent his final years in Edmonton and is buried in the churchyard of All Saints Edmonton on Church Street, a connection the area holds quietly but with good reason to be proud of. Pymmes Park, covering 53 acres along the western edge of the postcode, was once a royal hunting ground in the time of Henry VIII and today offers a lake, formal gardens, and open sports facilities that draw families from across the borough throughout the year. Edmonton Green Shopping Centre serves as the main commercial hub, while Fore Street's food market and independent shops reflect the genuinely mixed communities that have settled across N18 over the decades. Silver Street and Edmonton Green railway stations give the area its principal rail connections, with services running into Liverpool Street — useful for the City, but of no help when you need to reach Heathrow with a suitcase in hand.
