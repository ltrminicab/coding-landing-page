Train via London

Train travel from Gatwick to Orpington requires a journey into a central London terminal and a separate Southeastern connection out to BR6. Black Cab

Black cabs from Gatwick can travel to Orpington on the meter, but the fare is unconfirmed at the point of boarding and typically ranges between £75 and £110 depending on traffic and the time of day. Uber
