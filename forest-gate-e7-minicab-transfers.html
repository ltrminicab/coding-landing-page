Discover Forest Gate E7 – West Ham Park, Elizabeth Line Connections, and a Rich Multicultural Community

Forest Gate E7 is one of east London's most characterful and culturally rich postcodes, sitting within the London Borough of Newham with strong community roots and fast-improving transport connections. At the southern edge of E7, West Ham Park is one of the finest Victorian public parks in east London, covering 77 acres and managed by the City of London Corporation since 1874. Its formal gardens and historic character set it apart from other east London parks and attract visitors from across the borough and beyond.

Green Street, which runs along the southern boundary of E7 into E6 and E13, is one of London's most celebrated South Asian shopping corridors, renowned for bridal wear, sari shops, jewellery boutiques, and specialist food retailers. Wanstead Park Overground station on the northern boundary gives E7 residents an additional rail connection serving the Gospel Oak to Barking line.
