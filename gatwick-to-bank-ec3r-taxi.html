Gatwick Airport to Bank EC3R – Choosing the Right Way to Travel

Several transport options connect Gatwick Airport to Bank EC3R. Here is how each one performs in terms of journey time, price certainty, and convenience for a City of London arrival with luggage.


Gatwick Express

The Gatwick Express reaches Victoria in 30 minutes but does not serve Bank EC3R. City-bound passengers must then take the Victoria line to Bank or the Circle line to Cannon Street, adding a 15 to 25 minute Tube connection with airport bags across two or three interchanges before reaching EC3R.

Black Cab

Black cabs are available from the Gatwick taxi rank and will travel directly to Bank EC3R on the meter. The 28-mile City journey can cost £85 to £130 or more depending on traffic, time of day, and route, with no pre-agreed fare and no flight monitoring or arrivals service provided.

Uber

Uber operates at Gatwick but pricing increases sharply during morning business arrivals and peak hour slots into the City. There is no dedicated meet and greet, no flight monitoring, and the quoted price at the time of request is subject to change before a driver confirms.
