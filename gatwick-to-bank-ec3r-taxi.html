The Gatwick Express terminates at Victoria — not Bank. Reaching EC3R requires a transfer to the Circle or District line and continuing east to Monument or Bank. With airport luggage the full journey typically takes 70 to 90 minutes including waiting time and interchange.

Licensed black cabs queue at Gatwick's taxi ranks but run on a meter — the 28-mile fare to Bank EC3R typically falls between £85 and £130 depending on traffic and time of day, with no pre-agreed price and no flight monitoring included.
