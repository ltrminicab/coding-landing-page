The A23 is the most direct road corridor from Gatwick into the heart of the City of London, avoiding the western motorway network and delivering passengers into EC3R without unnecessary diversion. The Gatwick Express terminates at Victoria — not Bank. Reaching EC3R requires a second journey on the Circle or District line eastbound to Monument, adding 25 to 35 minutes including waiting and interchange. With airport luggage the total door-to-door time is typically 70 to 90 minutes.
