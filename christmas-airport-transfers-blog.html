Christmas Airport Transfers in London: When to Book and How Early to Leave

Most Christmas travel plans are sorted weeks ahead. The flights are booked, the presents are bought, someone has agreed to feed the cat. Then, a few days before departure, comes the question nobody has answered: how are we actually getting to the airport, and what time do we need to leave the house?

Late December changes the journey to every airport in the country. Terminals are fuller, roads are less predictable and, on some days, the trains do not run at all. Whether you need a London airport taxi for a dawn flight from Gatwick or a car to meet relatives landing at Heathrow, this guide to Christmas airport transfers explains when to book, which days are likely to be hardest and how to work out your leaving time. It covers Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton, London City and Southend, and most of the advice holds for airports anywhere in England.

Why Festive Travel Needs a Different Plan

Three things change at once in the second half of December.

Airports are at their busiest. Ahead of Christmas 2025, Heathrow said it expected its busiest December on record and its busiest Christmas Day ever. Other airports face the same seasonal rush, with longer lines at bag drop and security and plenty of passengers who fly only once a year.

The railways close. No trains run on the national rail network on Christmas Day. That rules out the Heathrow Express, Gatwick Express, Stansted Express and the Thameslink route to Luton in one go. In London, the Tube, Elizabeth line, DLR and buses have also stopped on 25 December in recent years. Boxing Day brings back only a handful of services, and engineering work frequently shuts key lines for days afterwards. Published plans for 2026 show work around Paddington from 27 to 29 December, which affects rail links to Heathrow. Check Transport for London's festive travel page and National Rail before relying on a train.

The weather joins in. Fog, frost and the occasional snowfall slow the roads and can delay flights while aircraft are de-iced.

None of this makes London airport transfers difficult in December. It simply means that everyday habits, such as booking a car the night before or leaving the usual amount of time, stop being reliable.

When to Book an Airport Taxi for Christmas

The short answer is: when you book your flights, or as soon afterwards as you remember.

There is a practical reason for this. On an ordinary Tuesday in March, a private hire firm can usually find you a car at a few hours' notice. Over Christmas, demand rises while the number of drivers on the road falls, because drivers have families and plans of their own. The dates that fill first are the ones with no alternative. A Christmas Day airport taxi is the clearest example, since on 25 December there is usually nothing else to fall back on.

How Early to Leave for the Airport at Christmas

Start from the flight and work backwards. There are three numbers to subtract.

Step One: When to Be at the Terminal

The usual guidance at UK airports is to arrive about three hours before a long-haul flight and two hours before a short-haul one. Heathrow gives the same advice in its guide to timing your journey and warns against turning up far earlier, since most bag drops only open around those times. Your airline's instructions take priority if they differ.

Step Three: Getting Out of the Door

Add ten minutes for loading the car. A family of five with seven bags does not leave the house in thirty seconds, and that time has to come from somewhere.

Three Worked Examples

Heathrow, on the big getaway weekend. Your long-haul flight leaves Terminal 5 at 10:30 on Saturday 19 December, and you live in Kensington. Be at the terminal by 07:30. Allow 1 hour 15 minutes for the drive and ten minutes to load. Book the car for 06:05.

Gatwick, for an early European flight. You are flying from the North Terminal at 07:05 and travelling from Croydon. Be there by 05:05. The roads are quiet at that hour, so 45 minutes is enough. Book the car for 04:10. Hardly any trains run that early at any time of year, which is why dawn flights and booked cars tend to go together.

Stansted, on Christmas Day itself. A 13:00 short-haul departure, starting in Stratford. Be there by 11:00. Traffic on Christmas morning is light, so allow an hour and book the car for 09:50.

Getting to the Airport on Christmas Day With No Trains

It comes down to the road. The choices are a lift from someone willing to leave the turkey, your own car plus the airport's parking charges, a coach on the few routes that operate, or a private hire vehicle reserved ahead of time. Black cabs and app-based cars do work on 25 December, but far fewer drivers are out, and availability at short notice is uncertain. Whichever you choose, arrange it before Christmas week.

Small Things That Save Time on the Day

  • Check your terminal. Heathrow has four in use, and Gatwick has two. Arriving at the wrong one can cost half an hour.
  • Leave presents unwrapped. Security staff may need to open them, so wrap gifts at your destination.
  • Ask before packing crackers. Some airports and airlines do not allow them at all.
  • Know the liquids rule at both ends. Heathrow now lets containers of up to two litres stay in your bag, while some other airports still apply the 100ml limit.
  • Check your flight before you set off. In freezing or foggy weather, look at the airline's app first. If the time moves, tell us and we will move the car.
  • Consider a hotel for a very early flight. A night near the airport turns a 3 am start into a 5 am one. See hotel transfers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book a Christmas airport transfer?
The day you confirm your flights is ideal. For the peak dates either side of Christmas and New Year, try to reserve two to three weeks ahead at the latest, and sooner if you need an MPV.

Are London airports open on Christmas Day?
Heathrow and Gatwick operate on 25 December with fewer flights. Stansted and Luton stay open but are very quiet, because several low-cost airlines do not fly that day. London City normally closes. Always confirm with your airline.

Is there public transport to London airports on Christmas Day?
Very little. There are no national rail trains, and London's Tube, DLR and bus networks have shut down on that date in past years. A small number of coaches may run and need booking ahead.

How early should I get to the airport at Christmas?
Aim for three hours ahead of a long-haul departure and two hours ahead of a short-haul one. If your airline asks for longer, go with the airline.

What happens to my booking if my flight is delayed or cancelled?
For arrivals, we track the flight and adjust the pick-up time at no extra cost. For departures, let us know as soon as the airline tells you, and we will change the booking.
